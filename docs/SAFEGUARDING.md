# Safeguarding Policy

Applies to staff, volunteers, visitors, and learners in all hub activities. Coordinators: Fatima Zoubi and Ahmad Arrabee. Review each academic term and after concerns.

## Supervised sessions

A designated facilitator must be present throughout every session, including arrival, shared-device use, breaks, and departure. Do not open or continue a session without supervision. Keep activities in visible shared spaces, avoid secluded one-to-one contact, and use a second authorized adult where possible. Follow locally agreed arrival/collection arrangements and do not leave a child alone waiting for collection.

## Conduct

Treat every learner respectfully and fairly. Harassment, bullying, discrimination, sexual misconduct, exploitation, threats, corporal punishment, and humiliation have zero tolerance. Maintain appropriate boundaries: no private messaging with children, secret meetings, requests for favors, or unauthorized photography. Use approved local communication arrangements with families. Volunteers must receive this policy and understand reporting before working with learners; any staffing checks must be completed under the local team's applicable requirements.

Educational participation never depends on publicity permission. Follow the [media permission template](MEDIA_PERMISSION_TEMPLATE.md) and protect learner accounts and records. Do not promise absolute secrecy about a safety concern.

## Raise a concern

Students, parents, and volunteers may speak privately to **Fatima Zoubi** or **Ahmad Arrabee**, directly at the hub or through the facilitator arranging an in-person conversation. No phone number or email is published here; the local team must make current contact arrangements available at sessions. If the concern involves one coordinator, report to the other. If neither can act safely, seek an appropriate independent local safeguarding authority.

Listen calmly, take concerns seriously, avoid leading questions, and record the person's words and essential facts accurately in a restricted local record. Report promptly to a coordinator; do not investigate or confront the alleged person yourself. Share information only with those who need it to protect the learner. Do not retaliate against anyone raising a concern.

For immediate danger, secure the learner's safety and contact local emergency services without waiting for an internal review. Coordinators arrange appropriate protective action, including removing an individual from learner contact while a concern is assessed, and referral to competent local services where required. Record actions and follow-up privately; keep families informed when doing so is safe. Do not publish allegations or student identities in repository issues or public reports.
