# Privacy Policy

Applies to the Northern Jordan Offline Village Learning Hub during the 2026 academic term. Policy owner: Ahmad Arrabee. Review at the start of each term and after any incident or system change.

## Information collected

- Non-identifying learner handles for signing in; do not use full names, phone numbers, national identifiers, or addresses as handles.
- Exercise and lesson progress, quiz attempts, and activity records stored by Kolibri.
- Attendance timestamps recorded by facilitators for session planning and aggregate reporting.

Handles are pseudonyms: progress and attendance can still identify a learner to someone who knows the handle. Keep any necessary handle-to-family lookup separate, local, and accessible only to authorized facilitators. Avoid collecting additional personal information; use aggregate counts in public reports. The coding page itself does not implement a submission database; any facilitator submission log follows this policy.

## Local storage and transmission

Student records stay on the server's local SSD and physical USB backups only. No student records may be transmitted to cloud storage, online analytics, remote support, email, or public GitHub repositories. Keep the learning network offline with no WAN connection, port forwarding, or automatic cloud synchronization. Software/content preparation must happen separately from learner records. Public README badges are external resources; use the local portal during sessions.

## Access controls

Ahmad Arrabee administers the system. Authorized local facilitators may access only the learner records needed for teaching, attendance, and support. Give learners individual accounts and facilitators individual staff accounts; restrict administrator privileges, protect credentials, lock the server screen, and keep the server and USB drives secured. Do not expose backup files through the HTTP document root. Review staff access each term and remove it when someone leaves.

## Retention and deletion

Retain identifiable progress and attendance for the current academic term and up to 90 days after its end for corrections and transition, then delete or irreversibly aggregate it. A parent or learner can request access, correction, or deletion through the session facilitator or Ahmad Arrabee; verify the request locally and complete it within 30 days. Explain any exceptional safeguarding record retention separately and limit access.

Delete the live account and associated records using the installed Kolibri version's supported administration tools; check that deletion covers the requested data rather than merely disabling login. Remove separate attendance/lookup records too. Existing snapshots may retain deleted data for at most 90 days; expire affected snapshots securely and keep a restricted local deletion log so restored backups do not reintroduce deleted accounts. Do not retain old snapshots indefinitely. Retain anonymous aggregate statistics only when individuals cannot reasonably be identified.

## Concerns and incidents

Ask the local facilitator to contact Ahmad Arrabee in person. Report lost USB drives, unintended access, or exposed records immediately. Isolate the affected device, secure remaining copies, record facts privately, and arrange appropriate notification to affected families. See [backup procedures](BACKUP_AND_RECOVERY.md) and [safeguarding](SAFEGUARDING.md).
