# Pilot Phase Report — 2025 Active Term (September 7 – December 13, 2025)

**Status:** Active Operations (Northern Jordan) | **Repository Documentation Published:** October 2026  

The Ta3alam (تعلّم) offline learning hub is a community educational initiative serving 42 registered learners across 18 households in Northern Jordan. This report documents the 14-week active pilot term conducted from September 7, 2025, to December 13, 2025. While software toolsets and evaluation frameworks were formalized and published in this repository in October 2026, all operational logs, attendance registers, and pre/post assessments were recorded during the live 2025 term. During operations, learner records were stored locally under pseudonymous handles with restricted access. Public reporting uses aggregate figures. The [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md) requires identifiable records to be deleted or irreversibly aggregated within 90 days after term end, with affected backups expired as specified there. This report does not establish that identifiable 2025 records remain available.

## Project-Team-Reported Educational Progress & Participation Impact (Ta3alam Pilot)

### 1. Student Engagement & Retention Rate

- **Active Learner Rate:** 85.7% (36 out of 42 registered students log in and participate regularly on a weekly basis).
- **Pooled Session Attendance:** 88.5% (892 attended learner-sessions out of 1,008 expected learner-sessions across the pilot term). This is a pooled ratio, not an unweighted average of weekly percentages.
- **Student Retention Rate:** 92.9% of enrolled learners (39 of 42 students) remained enrolled through term end.

### 2. Reported Learning Volume & Exercise Completion

- **Average Weekly Learning Volume:** 128 average weekly learning hours recorded via Kolibri engine activity logs. This is aggregate weekly learner time, not a whole-term cumulative total.
- **Student Average:** 3.56 active learning hours per student each week, using the 36 active weekly learners as the denominator (128 / 36, approximately 3.56).
- **Exercise Mastery:** 312 completed exercises (distinguished from incomplete attempts) across Arabic Math, General Science, and Logic.
- **In-Browser Coding Lab Submissions:** 145 successfully completed interactive JavaScript exercises.

The 312 completed subject exercises and 145 successful coding submissions are separate measures. These updated completion counts supersede the earlier broad report of 310+ coding submissions and 120+ weekly learning hours. The browser coding lab does not persist submissions automatically; its completion records require a separate local facilitator log.

### 3. Measured Educational Growth (Pre/Post Baseline Assessments)

To evaluate academic progress, learners completed short baseline skill assessments prior to the program, followed by matching end-of-term evaluations:

- **Mathematics (Algebra & Geometry Concepts):** +24.5% reported pre/post score improvement (81% of students demonstrated measurable score growth).
- **Science (Physics & Natural Phenomena):** +19.2% reported pre/post score improvement (76% of students improved).
- **Introductory Computer Literacy & Logic:** +38.0% reported pre/post score improvement (92% of students achieved passing proficiency).



The project team reports 36 matched pre/post test pairs. The repository does not document subject-specific denominators, baseline/endline means, the passing threshold, or whether each improvement is a relative change in group means or an average of individual relative changes. The percentages are therefore reported as supplied, not as independently reproducible calculations. The 92% figure describes reported passing proficiency, not a separately established mastery standard.

### 4. Technical Uptime & Data Reconciliation

- **System Uptime:** 99.2% operational availability maintained by Ahmad Obeidat during active learning hours.
- **Data Integrity:** 100% successful USB data backups (24 total snapshots executed) with zero data loss.
- **Reconciliation Procedure:** The project team reports that Fatima Zoubi and Nour Momani cross-checked physical attendance records against internal Kolibri logs. This cross-check does not independently establish complete accuracy of every reported metric.


## Operational breakdown

A previously reported 65% Kolibri / 35% coding-lab activity split is excluded from the pilot results because its denominator and measurement period were not documented.

## Evidence availability and limits

These are project-team-reported results, not an independent audit. Assessment questions, rubrics, anonymized score summaries, calculation worksheets, and historical operational logs are not included in this repository. The 107,520-minute whole-term figure is derived from 128 average weekly hours × 14 weeks × 60; it is not a separately supplied raw-log total. The 24 reported completed USB snapshots describe the historical pilot count; the daily backup schedule documented elsewhere is an operating procedure and does not establish a daily backup history for 2025. Do not reconstruct or publish learner-identifying records to support these figures.

Facilitators supervise sessions, support learner accounts, and maintain shared-device cleanup and physical USB backups. See [operations](OPERATIONS.md) and [safeguarding](SAFEGUARDING.md).
