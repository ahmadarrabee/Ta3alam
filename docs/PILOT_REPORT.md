# Pilot Phase Report — 2026 Academic Term

**Phase: active operations.** The Ta3alam hub is an operating educational service in Northern Jordan, reaching 18 families during the 2026 academic term. The verified metrics and reconciliation protocol below were supplied by the project team for this update. Underlying learner records and assessment data remain local under the [privacy policy](PRIVACY.md); they are not published or independently audited in this repository. A precise reporting window and cutoff date were not supplied.

## Verified Educational Progress & Participation Impact (Ta3alam Pilot)

### 1. Student Engagement & Retention Rate

- **Active Learner Rate:** 85.7% (36 out of 42 registered students log in and participate regularly on a weekly basis).
- **Attendance Consistency:** 88.5% average weekly session attendance across scheduled community center blocks.
- **Student Retention Rate:** 91.6% of enrolled learners remained actively engaged over the entire pilot term without dropping off.

### 2. Verified Learning Volume & Exercise Completion

- **Cumulative Learning Volume:** 128 average weekly learning hours recorded via Kolibri engine activity logs. This is aggregate weekly learner time, not a whole-term cumulative total.
- **Student Average:** 3.55 active learning hours per student each week, using the 36 active weekly learners as the denominator (128 / 36, approximately 3.55 as reported).
- **Exercise Mastery:** 312 completed exercises (distinguished from incomplete attempts) across Arabic Math, General Science, and Logic.
- **In-Browser Coding Lab Submissions:** 145 successfully completed interactive JavaScript exercises.

The 312 completed subject exercises and 145 successful coding submissions are separate measures. These updated completion counts supersede the earlier broad report of 310+ coding submissions and 120+ weekly learning hours. The browser coding lab does not persist submissions automatically; its completion records require a separate local facilitator log.

### 3. Measured Educational Growth (Pre/Post Baseline Assessments)

To evaluate academic progress, learners completed short baseline skill assessments prior to the program, followed by matching end-of-term evaluations:

- **Mathematics (Algebra & Geometry Concepts):** +24.5% average individual score improvement (81% of students demonstrated measurable score growth).
- **Science (Physics & Natural Phenomena):** +19.2% average score improvement (76% of students improved).
- **Introductory Computer Literacy & Logic:** +38.0% average score improvement (92% of students achieved passing proficiency).



### 4. Technical Uptime & Data Reconciliation

- **System Uptime:** 99.2% operational availability maintained by Ahmad Obeidat during active learning hours.
- **Data Integrity:** 100% successful USB data backups (24 total snapshots executed) with zero data loss.
- **Verification Protocol:** Physical attendance logs collected by Fatima Zoubi and Nour Momani were cross-reconciled against internal Kolibri database logs to ensure 100% data accuracy.
